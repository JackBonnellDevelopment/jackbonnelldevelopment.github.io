Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how JackBonnell.dev uses cookies and similar technologies.

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Jack Bonnell. For privacy questions, contact via the details on the About page.

What data is collected

This site can use Google Analytics 4 (G-2SNGTXSFNT) to understand how visitors use the site (for example page views, approximate location, device/browser type, and referral source).

Analytics cookies are not set until you choose Accept on the cookie banner. If you choose Reject, Google Analytics storage remains disabled via Google Consent Mode.

IP addresses are anonymized where supported by the analytics configuration.

Cookies

When you accept analytics, Google Analytics may set cookies or use local storage to distinguish visitors and measure traffic. These are used for analytics only, not for advertising on this site.

Legal basis

Analytics run only with your consent (Accept on the cookie banner). You can change your mind later using the cookie settings control shown after you make a choice, or by clearing site cookies in your browser.

How long data is kept

Google Analytics retention follows the settings configured in the Google Analytics property (typically months, not indefinite).

Your choices

You can:

  • Choose Accept or Reject on the cookie banner
  • Revoke consent using the cookie settings control (shown after a choice is made)
  • Clear site cookies in your browser
  • Use Google’s opt-out tools where available

Third parties

Google processes analytics data as a processor/service provider under Google’s terms. See Google’s privacy policy for details.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
